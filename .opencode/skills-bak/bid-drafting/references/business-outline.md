1. Cover + bid form (as required by tender)
2. Company profile
3. Compliance statements / legal commitments
4. Project schedule (工期) + milestones
5. Warranty & maintenance
6. Support SLA (response time, support window)
7. Delivery / installation / training plan
8. Quality assurance & acceptance
9. Required certificates & attachments checklist

Adapt to the tender’s required structure and tables.
